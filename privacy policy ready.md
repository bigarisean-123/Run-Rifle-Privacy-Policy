Run & Rifle ("the Game") is a single-player arcade game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, used, and shared when you install and play the Game.

## 1. Data Collected Directly by the Game
The Game does **not** require user account registration, nor do we request or collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location. 

* **Local Game Data:** Your gameplay progress (such as coins, skin unlocks, and settings) is stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not transmit or host your save data on remote servers.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Game displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve relevant ads, perform ad frequency capping, prevent fraud, and analyze ad performance, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:

* Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or IDFV).
* IP address and approximate (non-precise) location data derived from IP.
* Diagnostics, crash reporting, and ad performance data (such as views and clicks).

For more information on how Google processes and uses your data, please review:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information From Apps That Use Their Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 3. User Consent & Privacy Choices

### Ad Personalization & Consent
In regions where required by law (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, and California), you will be presented with a consent banner upon first launching the Game to choose between personalized and non-personalized ads.

### Opting Out / Resetting Advertising ID
You can modify your ad tracking preferences or reset/delete your device's Advertising ID at any time through your device settings:
* **Android:** Go to `Settings` → `Privacy` → `Ads` → Select **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
* **iOS:** Go to `Settings` → `Privacy & Security` → `Tracking` → Toggle off tracking permissions for the Game.
